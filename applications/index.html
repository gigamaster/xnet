Self-Host Applications

Self-host open source applications

Docker

Run apps in isolated, portable containers. Ideal for developers who want full control over their stack—from LAMP to modern microservices—with one command.

DevKinsta

Design, develop, and deploy WordPress sites from your local machine. DevKinsta is free forever, and available for macOS, Windows, and Ubuntu.

LocalWP (by Flywheel)

A polished, user-friendly WordPress development environment. Perfect for designers and freelancers who need staging, SSL, and push-to-cloud workflows.

Mamp

Launch Apache, MySQL, and PHP/Python on macOS or Windows with a simple GUI. Great for beginners learning web development or testing PHP apps offline.

XAMPP

A lightweight, open-source LAMP stack for Windows, macOS, and Linux. Best for quick local testing of PHP/MySQL projects—no setup required.

Self-Hosted PaaS

Deploy and manage apps with PaaS tools for security and efficiency, monitor containers, databases, and services with a visual dashboard.

General-purpose software categorized by function, with many offering both desktop (portable) and self-hosted server options: from personal dashboards to custom web apps. Includes self-hosted alternatives to SaaS platforms, often built with Docker, PHP, Node.js, or Python, and managed via tools like Dockge or Umbrel.

Communication

Private, decentralized alternatives to mainstream messaging, email, and video conferencing. Tools like Matrix/Element, Mailcow, and Jitsi enable end-to-end encrypted chat, secure email, and ad-free meetings without relying on Big Tech infrastructure.

Private & Secure Messenger Apps Alternatives
Signal

Messaging app with strong, open-source, end-to-end encryption. A phone number is still required for registration.

Threema

Switzerland-based messenger that does not require a phone number or email address, for superior anonymity and privacy.

Session

Anonymous, decentralized messaging app that does not require a phone number, using a Tor-like network.

SimpleX

Decentralized chat application that does not store any user identifiers, does not require a phone number.

Private Business & Team Communication Tools
Element (Matrix)

Alternative to Slack/Teams that ensures messages remain private via end-to-end encryption.

Rocket.Chat

An open-source, self-hosted platform for team communication with high security compliance.

ProtonMail

A secure email alternative to Gmail/Outlook that uses end-to-end encryption and a strict no-logs policy.

Passbolt

Open-source password manager audited and built for team collaboration. Made in Europe. Privacy by default.

Creative Software

Self-hosted tools for design, audio, video, and publishing. Includes photo editors, digital audio workstations, vector graphics, and collaborative whiteboards, often containerized for offline or LAN-based creative workflows.

Photo Editors and Management
Affinity

All professional photo, design, and layout software tools you need in one app, and free forever.

Immich

Self-hosted Alternative to o Google Photos,featuring automatic backups from mobile, AI face detection

GIMP

Portable/Desktop, open-source raster graphics editor, similar to Photoshop.

PhotoPrism

Self-hosted app for browsing, organizing, and sharing photo collections, leveraging AI for automatic tagging.

Design and Illustration
Excalidraw

Self-hosted virtual whiteboard for hand-drawn looking diagrams and sketches, with collaborative team sessions.

inkscape

Professional vector graphics editor for creating illustrations, icons, and diagrams.

Penpot

Self-hosted web-based design and prototyping tool that focuses on designer-developer collaboration.

Krita

Portable | Desktop for digital painting and illustration, offering advanced sketching and painting tools.

Audio Editors and Management
Audacity

Portable/Desktop The most popular open-source digital audio editor and recorder, known for its extensive plug-in support.

Ardour

Record, eEdit, and mix on Linux, macOS and Windows

Audiobookshelf

Self-hosted dedicated server for managing and streaming audiobooks and podcasts

Navidrome

Self-hosted lightweight music streaming server that works with your personal music library.

Video Editors and Hosting
Kdenlive

Portable | Desktop professional-level, non-linear video editor that supports multi-track editing and many formats.

Shotcut

Portable | Desktop cross-platform video editor with strong filter support and a customizable interface.

PeerTube

Self-hosted decentralized, federated video hosting platform (federation of servers) allowing users to host their own video sharing site.

AVideo

Self-hosted formerly YouPHPTube platform to host your own video sharing website, featuring video uploading, encoding, and streaming.
