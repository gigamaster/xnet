A suite of self-hosted tools that replace surveillance-heavy communication and productivity platforms, email, chat, calendars, documents, with private, encrypted alternatives you fully control.

Why Use It

  • End-to-end privacy: No third party scans your messages, emails, or files.
  • Data sovereignty: Your communications stay on your server, not in a foreign data center.
  • Resilience: Avoid service outages, censorship, or account bans from centralized providers.

Critical Practices

  • Always enable HTTPS (Tailscale Funnel or Let’s Encrypt).
  • Isolate sensitive services (e.g., email) from public exposure.
  • Back up encryption keys and user data regularly—your server is the only copy.
Best For

Remote teams, journalists, activists, families, and privacy-conscious individuals who refuse to trade personal data for “free” tools.
