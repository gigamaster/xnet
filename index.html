Core Principles

Privacy by Architecture means designing your system, or homelab, so that tools and data run on your own hardware. Your machine becomes the center of your network, not a node in someone else’s cloud.

Autonomy is the antidote to the invisible strings of social engineering baked into proprietary tools. By building on open-source foundations, you own the process and the output, pure utility, designed for creative mastery.

  • 1
    Sovereignty

    No surprise bills. Replace cloud lock-in with open-source tools that run on your hardware, on your own terms.

  • 2
    Mobility

    Work anywhere, offline or online. Your environment stays consistent across devices, with sync only when you decide.

  • 3
    Resilience

    Restore your setup in minutes from a USB drive or private Git repo. Safely share a local server using a tunneling service.
